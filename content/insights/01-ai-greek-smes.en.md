# AI has reached Greek SMEs, but not yet their daily work

Two surveys published in October 2026 both put AI use among Greek businesses at 47%.
Far fewer have made it part of normal operations with measurable results: 10% of SMEs with 10–50 staff.
The most useful finding is the gap between "we've tried it" and "we use it every day".

## Key figures

| Figure | Value | Source · date |
|---|---|---|
| SMEs (10–50 staff) using AI in the business | **47%** (39% in 2025) | AI Readiness Index 2026, EntersoftOne & ELTRUN AUEB, 350 SMEs · Insider.gr, 20 Oct 2026 |
| SMEs with substantive use (normal operation + measurable results) | **10%** (5% in 2025) | same · Insider.gr 20 Oct; SEPE 21 Oct 2026 |
| Readiness index (0–100) | **20** (15 in 2025, +33%) | same · Insider.gr 20 Oct; SEPE 21 Oct 2026 |
| See process automation as important vs. have adopted it | **54% vs 5%** | same · SEPE, 21 Oct 2026 |
| Businesses of all sizes using AI | **47%** (34% last year) | Strand Partners for AWS, 1,000 businesses + 1,000 citizens · Euro2day, 2 Oct 2026 |
| AI users at basic / transformative stage | **61% / 17%** (66% / 12%) | same · Euro2day, 2 Oct 2026 |

> **The two 47% figures come from different surveys with different samples** (350 SMEs of 10–50 staff vs 1,000 businesses of all sizes). They match by coincidence and should not be compared or combined.

## What it means

- **SMEs:** the first step is done. The next is to put AI into one specific process and measure whether anything changed. Training is the usual starting point: 1 in 10 have done it and 1 in 4 plan to.
- **Telecom and energy providers:** cloud use is rising (60%), and moving from trial to daily use depends on reliable connectivity.
- **Contractors and field-service firms:** job documentation, reports and customer correspondence are mostly text, the most widespread tool type in the AUEB survey (60% in regular use). AI inside ERP/CRM is still at 3%.

## What to watch

The next AI Readiness Index (can substantive use keep doubling?), training and funding schemes, cloud uptake, and whether transformative use (17%) keeps rising.
